Agent memory and learning loop

After each closed trade, DeepSeek reviews the entry context, AI rationale, fees, outcome and confidence calibration. The lesson is stored in PostgreSQL and the append-only artifacts/agent_journal.md, then injected into future decision prompts. Lessons are advisory: they can never change hard risk limits or enable leverage/shorting.
