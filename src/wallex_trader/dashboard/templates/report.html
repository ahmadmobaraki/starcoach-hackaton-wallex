Assumptions and limitations

Wallex does not provide historical L2 or trade-tape data. This backtest uses 1-minute OHLCV and a synthetic book with configurable spread and a conservative liquidity proxy. Fees are the measured TMN-market rates ({{ '%.2f'|format(fees_maker * 100) }}% maker / {{ '%.2f'|format(fees_taker * 100) }}% taker). Marketable entries and all exits pay taker fees plus slippage. When stop and target are both inside a bar, the stop is assumed first. No lookahead is used: decisions at bar close execute at the next bar open.
